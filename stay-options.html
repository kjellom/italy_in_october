Where to Stay — Sorrentine Peninsula & Agerola

Split into two two-week legs: one near the coast on the Massa Lubrense/Sorrentine side, one inland up in Agerola. All of these are self-catering with a kitchen — plus a few farmstays further down where meals are part of the stay instead.

Near the Coast — Sorrentine Peninsula

Massa Lubrense and Nerano side. All have kitchens for self-catering.

Photo: Google Maps listing

Relais del mare

★ 5.0 (21 reviews)

Sea-view terrace, free parking, 3–5 min walk to swimming and restaurants. Fully equipped kitchen. No official website — contact the host directly.

Inland — Agerola

Up in the hills above the Amalfi Coast, near the Path of the Gods trailhead. Cooler climate, closer to Positano/Amalfi/Ravello for day trips than Massa Lubrense is.

Photo: Google Maps listing
